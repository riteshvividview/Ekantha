venue features

what the estate provides.

Open Lawns
Wide, unhurried grass for anything from a badminton net to a two-hundred-person reception.
Covered Pavilion
A large open-sided pavilion, weatherproof for both peak sun and monsoon evenings.
Sound & Lighting Setup
In-house sound system and warm string lighting across the lawns and pavilion, ready without a rental truck.
Ample Parking
On-site parking for large groups, with attendants for events over fifty guests.
