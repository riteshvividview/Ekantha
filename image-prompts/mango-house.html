Mango House: image prompts

At the moment, Mango House borrows photos from other parts of the site. Each card below is one slot on the page. Attach the reference image shown, use the prompt, and save the result under the file name given.

For every image
  • Style: real photograph, natural daylight, warm and earthy colour, 35mm lens look, no text, no watermark, no people facing the camera unless the prompt says so.
  • Export as JPG, sRGB, 2400px on the long edge, quality around 80.
  • Put the files in C:\VividView Websites\Ekantha\image-prompts\mango-house\. I'll optimise them, upload them to R2 at images/site/ and connect them to the page.
  • Reference image: use it for the architecture, materials and palette. Don't copy its framing.
