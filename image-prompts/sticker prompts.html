How to use this

  1. Click Copy prompt and paste it into ChatGPT.
  2. Download the result as a PNG with a transparent background, 2000 px or larger. An SVG is even better if you can get one.
  3. Save it with the exact file name in the black tag.
  4. Put all 8 in web/public/images/organize/ and tell me.

Tips

  • If it adds a white border or outline, reply: "remove the white border and outline, flat silhouette only".
  • If it adds shading or texture, reply: "one flat solid colour only, no shading, no texture".
  • If the background isn't transparent, reply: "same image, transparent background PNG".
  • Don't use stock sites (Pngtree, Alamy, Depositphotos); their images carry watermarks and licences.
