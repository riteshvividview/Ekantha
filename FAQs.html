01

Check-in & Check-out

What are the check-in and check-out times?+
Check-in is from 14:00, once the held hours end and the farm receives guests. Check-out is by 11:00, so the cottages can be prepared for the next arrival.
Can I request an early check-in or late check-out?+
Yes, subject to availability. If the stay is free the night before or after, we're usually happy to accommodate — just ask when you hold your date.
What happens if I arrive before check-in time?+
The main house is open. There's chai, shade, and somewhere to leave your luggage while your cottage is readied.
Is there a minimum length of stay?+
Two nights, across all three stays. We've found one night is a preview, not a stay — the rhythm of the farm needs at least a second day to begin.
02

Booking Policies

How do I hold a date?+
Through the Hold a Date form — tell us your dates, who's coming, and which stay you'd like. A real person writes back within 24 hours to confirm.
Is a deposit required to confirm a booking?+
A 50% advance is requested to confirm a hold, with the balance due on arrival. No payment links, no surcharges added at checkout.
What is the cancellation policy?+
14 or more days before arrival: full refund. 7–14 days: 50% refund. Under 7 days: held as credit, valid for 18 months, rather than lost entirely.
Do rates change based on demand or season?+
No. We don't run dynamic pricing — the rate you see is the rate you pay, regardless of when you book or how busy the weekend is.
Can I change my dates after booking?+
Usually, yes — contact us as early as possible and we'll move your hold if the new dates are available.
03

Farm Dining Bookings

Do I need to be a staying guest to book Bamboo Farm Dine?+
No — Bamboo Farm Dine is open to day visitors as well as overnight guests. You don't need to be holding a stay to reserve a seat at the table.
How far in advance should I reserve a table?+
At least 24 hours, since the kitchen shops and cooks for the exact number confirmed. Weekends and celebrations are safer with a few days' notice.
Is there a fixed menu?+
No. The kitchen decides the same morning, based on what's ready to be picked from the garden. Dietary restrictions are handled if flagged in advance.
What happens if it rains during dinner?+
The dining hut has a full roof, so light rain changes nothing. In heavy weather, the table moves under the covered section beside the main house.
04

Event Bookings

How far in advance should I book an event?+
For birthdays and small gatherings, a couple of weeks is usually enough. For larger events — weddings, corporate offsites — we recommend 6–8 weeks to arrange catering and customisation.
What's included in a venue booking?+
Open lawns or the covered pavilion, in-house sound and lighting, and on-site parking. Catering, decor, and entertainment are added on top, based on what you choose.
Can I bring my own decorator or caterer?+
We prefer to run catering in-house, since it's built around the farm's own kitchen, but we're flexible on decor vendors — talk to us about what you have in mind.
Is overnight stay included with an event booking?+
Not by default — venue bookings are day-use unless you also hold Mango House, Stone House, or the Full House for the night.
05

Add-Ons

What add-ons are available?+
Farm dinners (₹800 per person), laundry, packed breakfast for early departures, and — for events — decor, catering, entertainment, and logistics add-ons like transport or a coordinator.
How do I request an add-on?+
Mention it in your note when you hold a date, or ask the caretaker once you've arrived — most add-ons don't need advance notice, though farm dinners are easier to arrange with a heads-up.
Are add-ons available to day visitors?+
Some are — farm dining and event customisations, for instance — while others, like laundry, are only offered to overnight guests.
06

Guest Capacity

How many guests can each stay accommodate?+
Mango House holds 2–3 adults, Stone House holds 2–4 adults across two bedrooms, and the Full House — the entire estate — holds up to 16 adults.
Can I bring more guests than the listed capacity?+
Only with prior approval, and additional guest charges may apply — ask us before you arrive rather than on the day.
Are children welcome?+
Yes. Mango House and the Full House are especially family-friendly, with open lawns and animals nearby to keep younger guests occupied.
Are pets allowed?+
Not currently. The farm keeps its own animals and a quiet rhythm we'd rather not disrupt — we hope to revisit this as the estate grows.
